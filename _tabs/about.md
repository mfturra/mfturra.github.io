## Olá :brazil:

I'm an engineer and software developer in a healthcare organization focusing on the integration of Cloud and AI vendor digital tools that improve clinical operational workflows. I'm deeply interested in transforming real-world challenges into virtual solutions. Lately, I've been working on open-source web tools designed to help university students in the U.S. better understand the often-overlooked costs of higher education. These tools use accurate, real-world data sourced directly from government websites to ensure transparency and reliability.

## About Me
With a Bachelors degree in Mechanical Engineering and a Masters degree in Bioengineering, I'm a tinkerer at heart—fascinated by how systems work both holistically and at the micro level. My biomechanics research and the entrepreneurial insights I gained from the Disciplined Entrepreneurship course at MIT Sloan inspired me to found a wearable tech startup, Extruo, aimed at bringing motion capture technology beyond the lab. That experience sparked a deeper curiosity about how healthcare institutions collaborate with startups, pilot new technologies, and design workflows that support innovation adoption.
